What it pulls out

contract_type
Classifies the document — loan agreement, lease, MSA, NDA — with a confidence level.
psr
Whether Payment Services Regulations apply, plus named parties and scope where relevant.
fees
Every charge, penalty, or rate mentioned, with what triggers it.
loss_clauses
Indemnity, liability limits, penalties, force majeure — with any stated cap.
dates
Effective date, expiry, renewal notice, termination windows.
conflicts
Clauses that contradict each other, flagged with severity.
